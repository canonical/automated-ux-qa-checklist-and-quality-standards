## Issues by page section

### Hero

**[Accessibility] 🔵 Minor – Video iframe title is too generic**
Evidence: `title="YouTube video player"`
The iframe title does not describe the video’s content, so it is less helpful for screen reader users. Update it to a specific title that explains what the video covers.

### Security compliance in action

**[Links] 🟡 Needs Work – Identical case study link text goes to three different destinations**
Evidence: "Read the case study ›" is used for Lucid Software, Grundium, and OEDIV, but each link points to a different URL.
This breaks the same-text-same-destination pattern and can be confusing when links are announced out of context or listed by assistive technology. Make each link text specific to the company or case study.

### What is NIS2?

**[Structure & Hierarchy] 🟡 Needs Work – Introductory content is presented as one long paragraph**
Evidence: the section contains a single paragraph of five or more sentences under "What is NIS2?"
This makes the content harder to scan and increases reading effort, especially on small screens. Break it into shorter paragraphs or a short intro followed by bullets.

### Ubuntu Pro supports NIS2 compliance everywhere

**[Links] 🔵 Minor – “Learn more” is still generic even with accessible labels**
Evidence: three visible links read "Learn more ›" for AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, each with a descriptive `aria-label`.
The ARIA labels cover the accessibility requirement, but the visible link text is still vague in context. Consider more specific labels such as "Learn more about Ubuntu on AWS."

### Take the next step towards NIS2 compliance

**[Links] 🟡 Needs Work – Certification link redirects to a different page than the label suggests**
Evidence: "Learn about all our security certifications" links to `/security/certifications`, which redirects to `/security/security-standards`.
The redirect may still reach relevant content, but it creates a mismatch between the promised destination and the final URL. Update the link to the final destination or confirm the redirect is intentional and user-friendly.
